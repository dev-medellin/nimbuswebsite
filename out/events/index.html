City War GUILD

A different city takes the spotlight each weekday.

City War is the nightly fight for control of a city pole. The guild that deals the most damage when the pole falls takes the city and its rewards.

  • When Monday – Friday · 20:00 – 21:00
  • Where The featured city for that day
  • Bring Your guild and your strongest build
